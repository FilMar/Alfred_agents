## Decision

Two things share the word "agent" in this repo, and they stay separate:

- **Skills** live in `skills/<name>/SKILL.md`, one per role. They are executed **inline**: read the `SKILL.md` and follow its protocol. A skill is never a value for `--hat`.
- **A delegated run** is a hat plus instructions, launched by `th run`. There is no roster and no file to create:

```bash
th run --hat black-core --task "<what to do>" --system "<who you are for this run>" --tools read,bash
```

A run's task text may ask it to *use* a skill ("use the christopher skill to retrieve…"). That is the run following a protocol, not the harness dispatching to a skill.

Members are gone — the file per delegate that held a role, a hat and a tool list. Those three things are arguments now, and `--system` is where a specific role goes, in front of the hat. The archive records the hat as the `actor` of a subtask.

## Why

A member was a file created to run one task, and then it stayed. Measured before the removal: `.th/members/` held **only test fixtures**, seven of them, regenerated by the suite every time it ran; `th history` showed hats invented on the fly. The ceremony had already been abandoned in practice — what survived was the cost of it.

Naming a delegate also created a second problem the hats do not have. A name like `knuth-black` claims a person's judgement and carries nothing: the actual behaviour came from the hat file plus a role sentence. Six hats, stable, mean experience accumulates somewhere countable — `tl` can now answer "what does black-core cost, and how often does it get used" because the actor is one of six values and not one of forty invented ones.

The distinction this replaces is unchanged in substance: a skill is read and followed, a hat is launched. Only the second half is rewritten, because the thing it named no longer exists.

Two consequences recorded here rather than by editing pages that describe them:

- `fury` is retired. Its whole job was generating a member roster for a project. [skill_router_pass_planned](skill_router_pass_planned) lists it among the skills to convert in the next pass — that entry has nothing left to convert.
- [agents_roster_lives_on_filesystem](agents_roster_lives_on_filesystem) stays live and untouched: it is about the **skill** roster, printed from the filesystem by `roster.py`, which never had anything to do with members.
