## Decision

The archive is a queue for deletion with six months of notice. It is not a neutral place.

An archived note is hidden from the default search. So it gets no hits. The only way back is an explicit search that includes archived notes. That search counts as a signal.

If nobody runs that search, the note stays under 0.2 and the refiner deletes it six months after `archived_at`. The log line is written first.

## Why

The archive looks like a safe store. It is not, and the user should know it before `--apply` is turned on.

The way back needs a person or a skill that asks for the archive on purpose. Without it, the hide step and the delete step form one slow path. That is acceptable: a note nobody looks for in six months is a weak one, and the log keeps its text. The two errors do not cost the same, and the six months and the log are the answer to that.

The option to include archived notes in `tb search` does not exist yet. It must exist before `--apply`, or the way back is closed.
