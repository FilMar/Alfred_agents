## Decision

This is an idea, not a rule. Nothing is changed yet.

`socrate` may stop being a skill. Its core, "ask questions to find out how much I understood", may become a general principle. A principle of this kind is a Third Identity rule, written by `mose`. The skill would then be removed.

## Why

The user never used the skill. They say the habit fits better as a principle that is always on than as a skill they must call.

What is lost if the skill goes: `socrate` also searched the Third Brain for contradictions and gaps in the user's thinking. `feynman` covers part of this, because it finds where an explanation breaks. The tb-based contradiction search has no other home. If the user wants it, it can be one line in the rule.

The order matters. Write the rule first. Remove the skill after. If the skill goes first, the principle is lost.
