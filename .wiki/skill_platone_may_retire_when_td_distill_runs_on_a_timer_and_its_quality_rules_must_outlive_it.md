## Decision

This is an idea, not a rule. Nothing is changed yet.

`platone` may be retired once `td distill --active` runs on a timer. The same holds for `aristotele`, once `tb link` exists.

Before `platone` goes, the rules of a good note in `note_quality.md` must find a new home. Otherwise they leave with the skill.

## Why

The distiller does the same work as `platone`: it extracts atomic concepts and saves them to the Third Brain. Two paths drift apart.

The distiller writes notes, but a search of `tools/` found no reference to `note_quality.md`. So the distiller does not read the quality rules today. The rules are the part of `platone` that has value after the skill is gone.

Two side effects of `platone` have no owner in the distiller:

- The serendipity challenge: a random note and a bridge. Phase 3 of the distiller looks for bridges by shape, which is a related but different step.
- Consolidation on a window of the `tl` archive, outside the pending queue.

Each of the two needs a choice: move it into the distiller, or drop it.
